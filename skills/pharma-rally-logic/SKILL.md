# Pharma Rally Logic

## Purpose

Use this skill to turn pharma/innovative-drug market materials into a structured rally-review report. The focus is stock-price logic, not full pipeline database construction.

The output should explain:

- why the sector or stock rose
- what catalyst or narrative drove the move
- whether the move is fundamental, event-driven, liquidity-driven, or sentiment-driven
- which stage the rally is in
- what signals suggest profit-taking or topping risk

Do not present unverified causes as facts. Label unsupported explanations as `待核验线索`.

## Core Framework

Analyze pharma stock rallies across six layers:

1. **Liquidity repair**
   Extreme valuation compression, low market cap, poor liquidity, and subsequent rebound.

2. **Narrative shift**
   Market reframes the sector, e.g. China innovative drugs as global competitive assets, AI healthcare, or BD globalization.

3. **Event catalyst**
   BD/licensing deal, academic meeting, clinical readout, NDA/BLA, approval, reimbursement, policy, or major company announcement.

4. **Fund rotation**
   Capital rotates between pharma, TMT, semiconductor, AI, robotics, or other high-beta sectors.

5. **In-sector diffusion**
   Typical order:
   - core institutional names lead
   - sentiment/high-beta small caps expand the rally
   - second/third-tier laggards catch up

6. **Risk/topping signals**
   - high-beta small caps top before the sector
   - sector starts underperforming while competing themes absorb capital
   - good news no longer lifts prices
   - expected catalysts become sell-the-news events
   - volume expands but price fails to hold

## Workflow

1. **Collect inputs**
   Use available sector index data, stock performance, company events, research decks, news, announcements, and meeting calendars.

2. **Define the observation window**
   Example windows:
   - 1 trading day for daily move review
   - T-10 to T+10 around an event
   - 1-6 months for sector rally replay

3. **Segment stocks**
   Group stocks into:
   - core institutional names
   - event-catalyst names
   - high-beta sentiment names
   - second/third-tier catch-up names
   - industry-chain names such as CRO/CDMO

4. **Map catalysts**
   Link the move to BD, ASCO/ESMO/AACR/WCLC, clinical data, policy, approval, or narrative change.

5. **Compare relative performance**
   Compare pharma/innovative-drug names with broader market, semiconductor/TMT/AI, and relevant pharma indexes where available.

6. **Classify rally stage**
   Use one of:
   - liquidity repair
   - narrative formation
   - event-driven acceleration
   - broad diffusion
   - late-stage catch-up
   - profit-taking / topping
   - post-catalyst verification

7. **Write the report**
   Use `references/report_template.md` when the user needs a structured write-up.

## Output Requirements

Include:

- sector summary
- key drivers
- representative stocks
- catalyst map
- rally-stage judgment
- risk/topping signals
- verification checklist
- next tracking points

Use tables where helpful. Keep causality disciplined:

- `已确认事实`: source-backed event or data
- `推断`: reasoned interpretation from price/event/flow patterns
- `待核验线索`: plausible but not yet source-backed cause
